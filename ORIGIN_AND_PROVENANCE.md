# COSMOS / CST — Origin and Provenance Record

## Cory Shane Davis / NavisWORLD

This document records the project story and the provenance boundary for COSMOS and Davis Cosmic Synapse Theory (CST).

It is intentionally written to distinguish **personal origin**, **project chronology**, **publicly inspectable evidence**, **technical implementation**, and **legal conclusions**. Those are not the same thing.

---

## The short version

COSMOS grew from a long-running effort by Cory Shane Davis to explore computational continuity: persistent memory, dynamic internal state, association learning, sensory context, recurrent world state, evidence, and adaptive loops that continue across interactions instead of resetting conceptually at every prompt.

The research timeline Cory uses is:

**2018 → 2024 → 2026**

- **2018:** conceptual/personal origin of the research direction.
- **2024:** formal CST research phase and the 12D framework.
- **2025:** increasingly executable memory, world-state, learning, and autonomous-study systems.
- **2026:** integration into COSMOS as a local-first runtime/platform with reproducible engineering boundaries.

The present repository is the source-first engineering implementation of that lineage.

---

## 2018 — the origin

The 2018 date is the beginning of Cory’s research story: the period in which the recurring questions behind COSMOS began to take shape around memory, state, recurrence, chaos, information, and continuity.

This should be understood as a **personal/project-origin statement**. It is not presented here as independent public proof that every mechanism later found in COSMOS already existed in executable form in 2018.

That distinction matters. A serious provenance record should say what a date actually proves.

## 2024 — Davis Cosmic Synapse Theory

By 2024, the research direction had been organized into **Davis Cosmic Synapse Theory (CST)** and a 12-dimensional computational/state vocabulary.

CST became the conceptual framework connecting several recurring project ideas:

- compact dynamic internal state;
- memory and state coupling;
- recurrent/chaotic dynamics;
- sensory or environmental context;
- association learning and plasticity-inspired mechanisms;
- information flow over time;
- measurable state transitions rather than purely narrative personality claims.

The repository links the research work identified as **The 12-Dimensional Cosmic Synapse Theory** and the related Zenodo record:

**DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.17574447**

A DOI and archived record are useful provenance anchors. They provide a persistent research reference. They should not be overstated as automatically proving patent ownership, worldwide priority, copying by another party, or scientific correctness of every theory contained in the work.

The next phase increasingly treated the ideas as engineering problems rather than only conceptual language.

Across the project lineage, work expanded into:

## What timestamps can and cannot prove

Repository commits, archived files, DOI records, releases, and signed/hashed evidence can help establish chronology.

They can support statements such as:

> “This specific artifact existed by this recorded date.”

They do **not automatically prove**:

- that another person saw the artifact;
- that another implementation was copied from it;
- that similar ideas are legally infringing;
- that a scientific claim is correct;
- that the artifact qualifies for patent protection;
- that all rights in every dependency or included file belong to Cory.

Those conclusions require their own evidence and legal/scientific analysis.

## Evidence discipline

COSMOS uses the following claim labels:

- `IMPLEMENTED`
- `OBSERVED`
- `MEASURED`
- `NULL`
- `HYPOTHESIS`
