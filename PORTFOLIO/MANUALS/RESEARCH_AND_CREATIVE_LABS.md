# Research & Creative Labs Manual

This section preserves exploratory work that is useful portfolio material but should not be confused with validated COSMOS architecture results.

## Navier–Stokes research conversations
Maintain hard boundaries among intuition, lemma, proved estimate, conditional result, unresolved obstruction, and complete proof.

Workflow: state exact 3D incompressible regularity target; fix spaces/solution concept/domain; derive available energy bounds; identify nonlinear term; track scaling/criticality; test proposed inequalities against known embeddings/interpolation; if closure fails, name the exact missing bound.

A Millennium Prize solution requires a complete independent-checkable proof. Brainstorming does not confer that status.

### Simple explanation
The hard question is whether a smooth 3D incompressible fluid can concentrate its own velocity gradients so violently that a singularity forms in finite time, or whether the equations always keep smooth data smooth under the target conditions. The child-level analogy is communication, not the proof.

## Creative AI Lab
These experiments ask what coherent artifacts emerge when AI is treated as a cross-medium collaborator.

### AI album / music
Lyrics, harmony/chords, arrangement, sonic palette, performance structure, browser instruments. Evaluation is artistic coherence/playability, not scientific truth.

### Creative-industry experiment
Long-form writing, screen/story structure, music, visual ideation, comedy, and technical prototyping through one workflow. The defensible claim is workflow breadth, not literal elimination of human creative work.

### Stand-up comedy
Long-form setup/payoff, callbacks, escalation, thematic continuity, transitions, persona. Human editing/performance/audience testing remain separate.

### Human vs. AI photography
Study plausible light, imperfect texture, optics/depth, asymmetry, material detail, composition, noise/context. A generated image can be photorealistic without being a photograph; provenance should remain clear.

## Why include creative work?
The same systems skills recur: real-time input, state, feedback, constraints, evaluation, iteration, tooling, and human-in-the-loop control. The evidence category differs.
