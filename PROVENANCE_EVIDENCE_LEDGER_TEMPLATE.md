## Evidence handling rules

1. Preserve originals. Work from copies whenever possible.
2. Hash original files before editing or converting them.
3. Keep at least two independent backup copies of important evidence.
4. Record timezone and timestamp source.
5. Do not crop away headers/metadata from the only surviving copy.
6. Export platform records using native export/download functions when available.
7. Preserve full conversation/thread context where lawful and relevant, not only favorable snippets.
8. Separate firsthand evidence from recollection.
9. Separate chronology from conclusions about copying, infringement, or intent.
10. Do not fabricate missing metadata or reconstruct dates from memory without labeling them as estimates.
11. Do not alter evidence to make it more persuasive.
12. Keep private evidence private unless disclosure is necessary and appropriate.
