# COSMOS Vision

**COSMOS is Cory Shane Davis / NavisWORLD’s owner-controlled, local-first experimental cognitive runtime and engineering research platform.**

Its research lineage is documented as **2018 → 2024 → 2026**: an early personal/conceptual origin, the formalization of Davis Cosmic Synapse Theory (CST), and the later integration of those ideas into an inspectable source-first runtime.

The project is built around a simple discipline:

> **Turn ideas into inspectable mechanisms, prove the mechanism is actually active, measure it against baselines, keep the nulls, preserve provenance, and never let the story become broader than the evidence.**

## Core principles

1. **Local first** — the core works without cloud AI or external credentials.
2. **Owner controlled** — Cory-owned original material remains under the permission boundary declared in `LICENSE`; third-party material remains under its own terms.
3. **Readable source** — supported releases should expose inspectable source rather than relying only on generated bytecode or opaque runtime state.
4. **Persistent but bounded** — long-term memory is durable storage plus retrieval and association, not infinite prompt context.
5. **Dynamic state is testable** — CST state mechanisms are computational hypotheses with preflight checks and controlled comparisons.
6. **Evidence before narrative** — implementation, observation, measurement, null, hypothesis, and metaphor remain distinct.
7. **Privacy by default** — raw private conversations, credentials, camera/audio, and health data do not belong in the repository by default.
8. **Fail soft** — optional model, quantum, sensor, or network services should not destroy the local core when unavailable.
9. **Autonomy is reviewable** — code generation follows proposal → branch/sandbox → tests → review → apply → rollback.
10. **Provenance is explicit** — the project should say what Cory created, what came from prior art or third parties, what a timestamp proves, and what it does not prove.
11. **Disputes are evidence-driven** — authorship or IP disputes should be preserved through exact artifacts, dates, communications, comparisons, and applicable rights rather than threats or unsupported conclusions.

## What COSMOS is trying to build

COSMOS explores whether persistent computational state, durable memory, association learning, sensory/context summaries, evidence receipts, and controlled feedback can form a useful continuity layer around model inference and tools.

The long-term goal is a modular research operating environment where memory, compact state, evidence, local inference, simulation, sensory summaries, creative systems, and reproducible experimentation share stable interfaces without collapsing into one untestable monolith.

## What COSMOS is not

- It is not evidence of machine consciousness.
- CST dimensions are not asserted as established literal physical dimensions by the software implementation.
- Quantum provenance is not automatically quantum performance advantage.
- A prediction UI is not proof of predictive skill.
- A repository timestamp is not automatically proof of copying or infringement by somebody else.
